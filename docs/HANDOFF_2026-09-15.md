### Phase 5 findings so far (40 held-out matches each)
- **Seeker:** trained (population mean) 0.77 vs Phase 4 explorer 0.66, p=0.22 (not significant). Brain seeker ≫ random (p=4e-9), ≪ scripted (0.66 vs 1.49). PFL3 silenced hurts (−0.17, p=0.04); LC10a silenced −0.15, p=0.14.
- **Hider:** best trained adapter survives 31.3 s vs 21.5 s explorer (p=0.015); population mean no gain. Scripted seeker still wins 39/40. PFL3 silenced hurts (13.1 s, p=0.01); silencing LC4/LPLC2 or DNp01 does not hurt.
- **Interpretation:** the connectome's steering circuit (PFL3) matters for both roles; CMA-ES over the adapter gives small gains at best. The limit appears to be the engineered policy / body (≈50% of match time in wall contact), not the tuned values.
